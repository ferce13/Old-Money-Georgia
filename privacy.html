Privacy Policy

Your discretion is valued. So is ours.

I

Introduction

Old Money Georgia ("we," "our," or "the Brand") respects your privacy with the same care we bring to our craft. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit oldmoney.ge. By using our website, you consent to the practices described herein.

II

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary and nothing more. When you join our waitlist, we collect your email address — provided voluntarily and with purpose. Through Google Analytics, we gather anonymous usage data: pages visited, time spent, device type, and approximate geographic location. This data contains no personally identifiable information. We also use essential cookies to ensure the website functions as intended.

III

How We Use Your Data

Your email address is used exclusively to notify you about our upcoming collections, brand updates, and launch announcements. Analytics data helps us understand how our audience interacts with the site, allowing us to refine the experience. We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing, nor do we share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

IV

Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic patterns. Google may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies. We use Netlify for hosting and form processing. Both services are industry-standard and maintain their own data protection practices. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to any other parties.

V

Cookies

Our website uses a minimal number of cookies: analytics cookies from Google to track anonymous usage statistics, and a functional cookie to remember your cookie consent preference. You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though this may affect certain features of the website.

VI

Data Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. Our website is served over HTTPS, ensuring encrypted communication between your browser and our servers. Access to collected data is restricted and handled with the discretion our brand demands.

VII

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to request correction or deletion of your data, to unsubscribe from our communications at any time, and to inquire about our data practices. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

VIII

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
